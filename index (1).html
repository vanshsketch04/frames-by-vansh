About

The mind
behind the frames

I'm Vansh — a filmmaker and motion designer obsessed with the intersection of UI/UX and visual storytelling. Every project starts with a single question: what do I want someone to feel?

With a background in both design systems and commercial production, I bring a rare lens to every brief — one that understands how products work, how users think, and how images move people. The result is film that's not just beautiful, but strategic.

Based in India. Working globally. Available for commercial projects, brand films, and UI/UX showcases.
